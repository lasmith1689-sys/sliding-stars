# Supplemental reference check — September 26, 2026

This supplements the [broader research](SLIDING-SEAS-BROADER-RESEARCH.md). It does not change the approved campaign scope or establish exact mechanic parity.

The US [App Store listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sliding-seas/id1436245163), checked during this research pass, listed version 2.4.7 dated September 9, with 50 new levels and buildings, decorations and VIPs in its release notes. A January 2022 player review praises small ambient animations and the simple interface but dislikes automatic hints arriving after 5–10 seconds. A July 2024 review describes continued engagement beyond 1,400 levels, including bottom-drop rescues and varied objectives. These are dated individual opinions; release history shows continuing content, not a measured inventory of every mechanic.

A search-indexed April 25, 2025 [Reddit reply](https://www.reddit.com/r/AndroidGaming/comments/1k72sf2) recommends Sliding Seas to someone seeking games that are easy to resume without daily-login pressure, describing optional ads. Direct retrieval failed with a cache miss. This is indexed-excerpt evidence, not a full-thread reading or representative player survey.

For Sliding Stars, these findings support continued home/VIP development, optional coaching and reliable short-session resumption. They do not justify adding ads, daily obligations, events or extra mechanics. The approved relaxed 1,000-level campaign with 25 distinct mechanics remains authoritative. Native reference-game timing, a complete current mechanic inventory and physical-iPhone performance remain unmeasured.

Three additional original pages were retrieved in a bounded community follow-up:

- A September 14, 2021 [Canadian App Store review by Mike Purvis](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/sliding-seas/id1436245163) reports finishing level 250, enjoying the varied modes and wishing to revisit preferred puzzle types. It also describes repeated attempts caused by random outcomes. This is historical evidence through 250, not current late-game completion evidence.
- A December 13, 2023 [Chilean App Store review by diego_saezp](https://apps.apple.com/cl/app/sliding-seas/id1436245163) appreciates returning occasionally and finding new mechanics built on familiar foundations. The player's reached level is unspecified.
- A January 7, 2022 [TapTap firsthand editorial](https://www.taptap.cn/moment/228586140196669568) connects the game's comfort with smooth tile merging and cheerful character reactions. Its enthusiasm for playing 1,000 levels is rhetorical, not a completion claim.

Our design inference is to vary actual decisions while preserving the familiar rescue/merge loop, identify unlocked puzzle types clearly, and make combinations and rescues visibly rewarding. These individual accounts do not establish exact unlock timing or a complete mechanic inventory. The follow-up found no additional verified late-game geometry account; the earlier visual evidence remains the basis for that requirement.
