Sliding Stars privacy policy · October 4, 2026

Data stays on your device

The iOS game does not collect, upload, sell or share personal data. It has no account, advertising, analytics or tracking. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

Mission progress, earned items, station decorations and preferences are saved locally on your device. We cannot access that save. Reinstalling the app or clearing its data may remove your progress. There is no cloud synchronization.

Playing offline

The iOS app bundles its game and artwork and works without an internet connection. The optional browser version downloads its offline files from Netlify. Website hosting may process ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, to deliver the site; the game does not use it for analytics or advertising.

TestFlight and support

If you use TestFlight, Apple handles beta installation, crash reporting and feedback under its own policies. If you choose to contact support through GitHub, your message and any information you include are handled by GitHub. Please do not post passwords, private keys or other sensitive information in a public issue.
