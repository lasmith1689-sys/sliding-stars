Welcome, star rescuer.

Sliding Stars is a cozy puzzle game about building habitats and bringing tiny travelers home.

How to play

Drag a terrain tile one square to a neighboring tile. Make a line of three or more matching terrain tiles to grow your little space habitats. Shuttles with guests aboard can fly one square toward a station entrance. Empty shuttles need a terrain match to move.

Tap the field guide for the rules on your current board, or ask for a hint. Missions advance automatically after the rescue celebration. Retries are free.

Saved progress

Progress is saved on your device. The iPhone app and browser game have separate saves. Do not delete the app or clear website data if you want to keep your current progress. Cloud backup and cross-device synchronization are not currently supported.

Get help

For a bug report, include your mission number, iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that led to the problem. Screenshots are helpful. Do not include private account details.

Contact support on GitHub

A GitHub account is needed to submit a support request. Existing reports are available without signing in.
